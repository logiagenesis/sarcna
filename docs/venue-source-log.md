## The wine estate question

The site states plainly that Boschendal is a historic Cape farm with a
wine-making history, and equally plainly that **no alcohol is served at any
convention event and no tasting is offered as a convention activity**. That
wording appears on the About page, the Venue page, the Venue history page and
the FAQ.

Two things the committee should confirm before launch:

1. **The physical layout.** Are the areas the convention uses separate from the
   estate's tasting facilities? The venue history page offers to describe the
   layout to anyone who asks — make sure someone can.
2. **The wording.** Some fellowships prefer a venue's wine history not to be
   mentioned at all; others prefer honesty about where people are going. The
   current wording chooses honesty. Change it if the committee decides
   otherwise.

## The valley's history

The venue history page acknowledges that the farms of the Dwars River valley
were built on the labour of enslaved people, and that the descendants of those
communities still live there. This is historically accurate and, in a South
African recovery context, worth saying rather than skipping past.

Have someone on the committee read that section before launch and confirm they
are comfortable with both its accuracy and its tone.
